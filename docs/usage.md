# Use the skills

After you [install the skills](install.md), ask your agent for what you need in plain English. The agent matches your request to a skill's description and loads it. You can also name the skill: "Use the creator-seo-metadata skill to..."

## Brief it like a colleague

The skills ask for missing details, but a complete first request saves a round trip. Include:

- the raw material: your idea, notes, script, or transcript,
- who your audience is,
- how you talk: paste a past script or post you like, or your voice profile,
- the platform and length,
- for pitches, your real, current stats with the date.

## Start with your voice

Run the `creator-voice-profile` skill once with two to five of your own scripts or posts. Keep the result and paste it at the start of later requests. Drafts match your voice much more closely when the agent has real samples.

## Requests that work well

- "Here are my notes for a video on fixing a squeaky office chair. Write an 8-minute script with three hook options."
- "Here's the transcript of episode 42. Write show notes with timestamps and pull six clip moments."
- "Give me title options and a description for this video. The main search phrase is `beginner watercolor`."
- "Turn this video transcript into a thread, two short posts, and an Instagram caption."
- "Plan my next four weeks. I can make one long video and three shorts a week, and the sponsor video must go up on the 18th."
- "Write a pitch to a yarn brand. My stats as of this month: `[your numbers]`."

## Package one piece of content

Ask for the `creator-content-agent` workflow, or say: "Take this idea from script to titles, posts, and a calendar slot." The agent stops after each step for you to approve or change it.

## Review everything before you publish

1. Read it out loud. If it doesn't sound like you, ask for changes ("more casual", "cut the hype").
2. Search for `[verify]`, `[your number]`, `[your story here]`, `[add link]`, and `[check current platform rules]` and fill them in.
3. Check every disclosure is there and clear.
4. Check titles and thumbnails match what the content really covers.

## Keep private details out

Don't paste guests' or viewers' personal details, private messages, or confidential brand terms. The skills tell the agent to leave them out, but by then the text has reached the AI provider.
