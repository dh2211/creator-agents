## Questions

**Warm-up**

1. What did a normal week look like when you were a sound engineer?
2. When did the idea of your own room first show up?

**Opening the room**

3. What did the engineering years teach you that you use every night now?
4. What surprised you most in the first month? Follow-up: what would you do differently?

**Booking**

5. How do you find local acts?
6. What makes you say yes to a band? Follow-up: and no?
7. How do you split the door with artists? `[verify with guest: does she want to discuss money on air?]`

**The room**

8. What does 60 seats make possible that 300 doesn't?
9. What's the hardest part of a small room?
10. Tell me about a night that went wrong.

**Closer**

11. One thing you'd tell your past self?
